Accessibility

Accessibility statement.

Our aim

We want everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.2, level AA), but the site has not had an independent accessibility audit, so we do not claim full conformance.

What we have done

  • The site works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a “skip to main content” link.
  • Text and background colours are checked for contrast, and the pages adapt to phones, tablets and large screens.
  • Photos have descriptions for screen readers, and menu icons are decorative so they are not read out.
  • The menu is available as normal web text, and the short video has no sound and does not play automatically.
  • Animations are switched off for visitors whose device asks for reduced motion.

Known limits

  • The downloadable PDF menu is not fully tagged for screen readers. The menu page has the same information in an accessible form.
  • The Google Map is provided by Google and may not be fully accessible. The address, directions link and phone numbers are on the page as text.

Tell us if something is difficult

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in another format, please call 01773 772554 or email info@sixhillscafe.com and we will help.
