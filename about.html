Our journey

Our journey in Selston began in 2020.

Over the years, this lovely village has become a very special place in our hearts.

Since then, we have been running our Nepalese and Indian restaurant, Gurkha Chulo, sharing our love of food and hospitality with the local community.

As we grew closer to the village, we dreamed of creating a cosy and welcoming place where families, friends and neighbours could meet, relax, enjoy a lovely cup of coffee and make happy memories together.

Our name

Named for the village we love.

We wanted a name that celebrated the village we love, so Six Hills Café was born. It’s our little way of bringing people together and giving something back to the wonderful Selston community.
