Privacy

Privacy and cookies.

Who we are

Six Hills Cafe, 137 Nottingham Rd, Selston, Nottingham NG16 6BT. You can contact us at info@sixhillscafe.com or on 01773 772554.

What this website collects

This website has no accounts, order forms or contact forms, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers. We do not set cookies of our own. If you email or phone us, we only use what you tell us to reply to you.

Google Maps

The Visit page can show a Google Map. The map does not load until you press Load map. Once loaded, Google receives your IP address and may set cookies, under Google’s privacy policy. If you load the map, your browser remembers that choice on this device so you are not asked again. Clearing this site’s data in your browser resets it. You can also use the Open in Google Maps button instead.

Fonts, photos and video

Our fonts, photos and the short video on this website are hosted on the website itself, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts, a photo library or any other outside service when you browse. The video has no sound and does not play until you press play.

Social links

The Facebook and Instagram links take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies. Nothing from them loads on our pages.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email us and we will help. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
